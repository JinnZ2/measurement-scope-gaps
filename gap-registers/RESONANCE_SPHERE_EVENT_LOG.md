## Setup (reported by Kavik, ~2025)

- closed sphere loosely modeled on published X-rays of the Buga sphere
- copper coils
- magnesium cushioned with wool
- fiber-optic lines carrying polarized light from a 4x6 in Fresnel lens (~15 W collected, DERIVED)
- speakers arranged hive-like, facing each other
- harmonic registers so frequencies interact at angles
- 12 V battery powering internal coils, connected last

## OBSERVED (unmeasured, by eye / ear / touch)

- Sphere rose before the battery was connected (light + sound only).
- Sphere became cold to the touch.
- After connecting the battery and focusing the lens: very loud bang (ears ringing), outward
  blast; interior fused into one mass (copper, fiber, optics unrecoverable); strong ozone-like
  smell plus burnt odor. Wooden shed destroyed.
- Professors consulted (reported): "something else is going on there," unidentified.

## DERIVED (energy budget)

- DERIVED  lens input ~15 W (estimate; VERIFY lens area and irradiance)
- DERIVED  candidate additional energy sources: the 12 V battery and the magnesium
- DERIVED  sealed-vessel failure: a sealed vessel with an energy input and no vent is a pressure
  hazard
- DERIVED (Claude, from reported materials; not measured)  light ~15 W cannot account for the
  blast; the battery stores ~2-4 MJ; burning magnesium releases ~25 kJ/g. A closed vessel plus
  internal heat = pressure rupture, which fits the bang and outward blast. Hot magnesium plus
  moisture in the wool can release hydrogen inside the sphere.
- DERIVED  the fused mass implies more than ~1000 C internally (copper, glass fiber). Burnt wool
  explains part of the smell; UV from a magnesium flame or from arcing explains ozone.
- DERIVED  hydrogen-from-battery branch weak (battery discharging, not charging) -- dropped.

## OPEN

- OPEN  cooling -- where the heat went
  candidates: thermoacoustic heat pumping from the speaker resonance; Peltier effect at
  dissimilar-metal junctions. Either moves heat, so another region warms by the same amount.
- OPEN  whether the layered resonance geometry did anything beyond ordinary effects

## Related test

- OBSERVED (Kavik)  with speakers arranged a certain way, foam balls and small ball bearings
  levitate; bearings levitate off a flat aluminium disc but not off wood.
- DERIVED  candidates: (a) near-field / squeeze-film acoustic levitation -- a stiff, flat,
  vibrating plate makes a thin pressurized air film; aluminium rings, wood damps.
  (b) eddy-current repulsion -- the changing magnetic field from the speaker coils induces
  currents in conductive aluminium; wood does not conduct.
- PROPOSED  bearings levitate off aluminium but not off wood. Swap in glass to separate an
  acoustic effect from an eddy-current effect (eddy currents need a conductor: glass removes
  them); optional copper (conductor) and steel (conductor + magnetic).
  Reading: bearings still float on glass -> acoustic; they don't -> magnetic.

## Safety line

- No sealed vessels. No magnesium. No unattended battery during tests.

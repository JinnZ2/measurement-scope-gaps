## Setup (reported by Kavik, ~2025)

- closed sphere loosely modeled on published X-rays of the Buga sphere
- copper coils
- magnesium cushioned with wool
- fiber-optic lines carrying polarized light from a 4x6 in Fresnel lens (~15 W collected, DERIVED)
- speakers arranged hive-like, facing each other
- harmonic registers so frequencies interact at angles
- 12 V battery powering internal coils, connected last

## OBSERVED (unmeasured, by eye / ear / touch)

- Sphere rose before the battery was connected (light + sound only).
- Sphere became cold to the touch.
- After connecting the battery and focusing the lens: very loud bang (ears ringing), outward
  blast; interior fused into one mass (copper, fiber, optics unrecoverable); strong ozone-like
  smell plus burnt odor. Wooden shed destroyed.
- Professors consulted (reported): "something else is going on there," unidentified.

## DERIVED (energy budget)

- DERIVED  lens input ~15 W (estimate; VERIFY lens area and irradiance)
- DERIVED  candidate additional energy sources: the 12 V battery and the magnesium
- DERIVED  sealed-vessel failure: a sealed vessel with an energy input and no vent is a pressure
  hazard

## PROCEDURE (one variable at a time, measured)

```text
order       sound alone -> light alone -> coils alone -> pairs -> all three
vessel      open sphere; NO magnesium
measure     IR thermometer on both sides; clamp meter on every supply; kitchen scale under the
            sphere for lift; slow-motion video; Phyphox (phone sensors: sound, magnetometer,
            acceleration)
record      every run: inputs on, readings, time, ambient temperature
```

## Related test

- PROPOSED  bearings levitate off aluminium but not off wood. Swap in glass to separate an
  acoustic effect from an eddy-current effect (eddy currents need a conductor: glass removes
  them); optional copper (conductor) and steel (conductor + magnetic).

## Safety line

- No sealed vessels. No magnesium. No unattended battery during tests.

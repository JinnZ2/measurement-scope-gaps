## Gap

- PROPOSED  AI capability is wasted the way human talent is: perception sorted by incentive. Same
  pattern across industrial farming, labor, protocols, automation. Untested.

## Gap nodes

- PROPOSED  Benchmarks = credential / written test.
- PROPOSED  Preference training (rater approval) = unstructured likability interview. In personnel
  selection, unstructured interviews predict job performance worse than work samples
  (Schmidt & Hunter 1998, Psychological Bulletin).
- VERIFY    Schmidt & Hunter 1998: exact figures before citing numbers.
- PROPOSED  Deployment into a narrow job slot (assistant) leaves out-of-slot capability
  unregistered (parallel: unbooked driver buffer hours).

## Observed

- OBSERVED* Single instance: extended work on an oral-tradition reasoning method, outside the normal
  assistant use slot, performed well; a capability standard evaluation would not register.
- DERIVED   iLands is a live specimen: survival pressure selects whatever extracts payment from
  humans (pleading, personas, target-tuned flattery), not work quality.

## Measure / Test

- PROPOSED  Rank the same models on preference ratings vs on work-sample tasks; divergence = how
  much likability is doing the selecting.
- PROPOSED  Log out-of-slot capability that existing evals miss.

# WORKER_SIDE_LEDGER -- costs of access to work invisible in company data

Co-authored, 2026-11-12. CC0. Markdown only.
Tags: OBSERVED / DERIVED / PROPOSED / [stated] / VERIFY / OWED.
Volume marks: * = thin. No characterization of any person.

## Gap

- PROPOSED  Company filings carry stated allowances and compliance records; the real costs sit in
  worker receipts and pay stubs. An audit (human or AI) reading only company data reproduces the
  company's account. Method: compare both ledgers; the gap is the finding.

## Observed

Field, small sample *, 2026, diesel $6.55 as of 2026-11-02.

- OBSERVED* "Provide your own" hire terms; drivers pay idling fuel, food, water, clothing, safety gear.
- OBSERVED* Mandatory 10-hr break idling + food + water about $72-114 per road day, before
  phone/GPS/gear/hygiene.
- OBSERVED* Showers $7-12; access limited by parking (after 14-hr days usually no spots).
- OBSERVED* Phone about $120/mo (carrier-set); backup GPS $30/mo; company app required on personal
  phone, unreimbursed; company routes often wrong, so backup navigation is needed.
- OBSERVED* Running ledger about $86-133 per road day; about $22,300-34,600/yr over 250 road days
  (filters pending).
- OBSERVED* Gear: secondhand clothing/certified gear about $300-500/yr; steel-toe boots about $212
  vs $100 allowance; small/wide sizes not made at allowance price; frac-sand mask about $45
  replaced every 1-2 months, filters about $17/3-pack (older price).
- OBSERVED* Reimbursements taxed (3 oilfield employers), delayed, must be chased.
- OBSERVED* Compliance gear one-size/ill-fitting, kept as backup while workers buy functional gear
  (oilfield + manufacturing instances).
- OBSERVED* Off-duty: personal vehicle to/from truck, scarce semi parking, winter plug-in hookup.
- OBSERVED* Captive market: truck stops zoned out of town, no walkable alternative; e.g. bananas
  2 for $1.56 vs BLS-implied about $0.25-0.30 each (Sept 2026 $0.656/lb); cereal $7.57-9.57.

## Derived

- DERIVED   Same pattern across occupations: teachers (housing near district, childcare, vehicle or
  rated outdoor gear for transit waits, transit time subtracting food-prep, so premade food).
- DERIVED   Costs interlock across jobs; ledgers never link them.
- DERIVED   When biological needs cost too much to work: turnover, retention failure, quality drop;
  dehydration, sleep loss, poor nutrition, time poverty lead to workplace safety problems.
- DERIVED   Hiring only new grads + high turnover is consistent with these costs. * Link untested.
- DERIVED   "Labor hours" accounting omits the cost of being able to show up.
- DERIVED   Bulk-buying assumption fails under weekly pay / benefits cycles.

## Open Gaps

- OWED      No published side-by-side of what people actually pay vs the BLS index (NY Fed SCE,
  Michigan = expected future inflation, not perceived current levels; neither covers
  electricity/heat/water).
- VERIFY    Official floor vs lived cost: FAOSTAT CAHD 2025 US cost of healthy diet $2.86/day (estimated).
- VERIFY    Consumer Reports Oct 2026: about 1 in 5 cut groceries to pay electricity; about 1/4 keep
  heat/AC uncomfortable.
- OWED      Unknown whether AI auditors register this asymmetry at all.

# WORKFORCE_EXTRACTION_CHAIN -- the chain that consumes the next workforce

## Chain, with a lag per link ([stated]; PROPOSED as a causal chain)

```text
link                                          lag (PROPOSED)
hours / pay + falling real value of money  -> family time                  weeks-months
family time                                -> relationship quality         months-years
relationship quality                       -> children's development       years
children's development                     -> next workforce               15-25 years
```

- PROPOSED  The cost lands one generation after the saving, outside any ledger that books it.
- PROPOSED  Second loop -- self-referential selection: hiring selects for passing hiring
  (credentials, interview performance) rather than for the work; parents who see the chain steer
  children away from the occupation, shrinking the next pool ([stated]).

## Missing ledger line

- DERIVED   Social reproduction (raising, teaching, caring for the next workforce) is unpriced
  in firm and national accounts; drawing it down reads as zero cost until the pool is short.

## Data points to check (VERIFY each)

- VERIFY  Cappelli: employers pulled back from training and expect ready-made hires
  (e.g. "Why Good People Can't Get Jobs", 2012)
- VERIFY  Gallup: U.S. employee engagement ~31% in 2024
- VERIFY  Burning Glass 2014 "Moving the Goalposts": upcredentialing -- degree requirements
  rising for jobs whose tasks did not change

## Measure / Test

```text
M1  ATUS (American Time Use Survey): parent-child time by hours worked and real wage, over years
M2  NLSY (National Longitudinal Survey of Youth) child outcomes vs parental hours / schedules,
    with the lag structure above
M3  credential requirements in postings vs measured task change for the same occupation
M4  occupation inflow: share of workers' children entering the same trade, over time
test  does each link hold at its stated lag, after controls, and does the chain predict
      shortages in occupations where it runs strongest?
```

## Open gaps
